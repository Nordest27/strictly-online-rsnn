Setup

Gaussian actor over the LIF membrane, matching the thesis's own indexing:

$$ v_j^t \sim \mathcal N(\mu_j^t, \sigma_j^2), \qquad \varepsilon_j^t = v_j^t - \mu_j^t, $$ $$ \mu_j^t = \alpha v_j^{t-1} + \sum_i W_{ji} z_i^{t-1} - z_j^{t-1} v_{\mathrm{th}}, \qquad z_j^{t-1} = H(v_j^{t-1} - v_{\mathrm{th}}). $$

Score function

$\log\pi(v_j^t\mid\mu_j^t,\sigma_j^2) = -\dfrac{(\varepsilon_j^t)^2}{2\sigma_j^2} + \text{const}$, so

$$ \nabla_{W_{ji}} \log \pi(v_j^t \mid \cdot) = \frac{\varepsilon_j^t}{\sigma_j^2} \cdot \frac{\partial \mu_j^t}{\partial W_{ji}}. $$

Differentiate through the reset

$$ \frac{\partial \mu_j^t}{\partial W_{ji}} = \alpha \underbrace{\frac{\partial v_j^{t-1}}{\partial W_{ji}}}_{=\ \partial \mu_j^{t-1}/\partial W_{ji}} \;+\; z_i^{t-1} \;-\; v_{\mathrm{th}} \underbrace{\frac{\partial z_j^{t-1}}{\partial W_{ji}}}_{\approx\ \psi_j^{t-1} \cdot\, \partial \mu_j^{t-1}/\partial W_{ji}} $$

(middle term: $\varepsilon_j^{t-1}$ doesn't depend on $W_{ji}$; last term: the surrogate $\partial z/\partial v \to \psi$, already defined in the thesis, applied to the reset's own Heaviside.)

Collecting terms, with $e_{ji}^t \defeq \partial \mu_j^t / \partial W_{ji}$:

$$ e_{ji}^t = \left(\alpha - v_{\mathrm{th}}\,\psi_j^{t-1}\right) e_{ji}^{t-1} + z_i^{t-1} $$

Result

Per-step policy gradient:

$$ \nabla_{W_{ji}} \log \pi(v_j^t \mid \cdot) = \frac{\varepsilon_j^t}{\sigma_j^2} \cdot e_{ji}^t. $$

Online actor update (same $\mathcal F_\lambda$ accumulation already used in the thesis):

$$ \Delta W_{ji} \;\propto\; \sum_t \delta_t\, \frac{\varepsilon_j^t}{\sigma_j^2}\, e_{ji}^t. $$

One trace, $e_{ji}^t$, with $\psi$ appearing exactly once, exactly where the reset is non-differentiable, and nowhere else. $\varepsilon_j^t/\sigma_j^2$ is used fresh at each step, not separately filtered. No $\xi_j^t$, no separate end-multiplication by $\psi$ — both are subsumed into $e_{ji}^t$'s own decay.
